Derma Brand Consumer Complaint Priority & Positioning Map

2025

Analyzed consumer complaint patterns across major derma cosmetic brands by collecting 2,197 negative reviews via Selenium. Applied PCA modeling to map brand positioning based on complaint factors, revealing gaps between marketing claims and actual consumer experiences.

Role: Data Collection & Modeling (Score Design, Web Scraping, PCA)

  • Collected 2,197 negative reviews (1-3 stars) from Olive Young using Selenium web scraping across 5 major derma brands.
  • Designed a 'Final Score' metric applying log-based reliability correction to mitigate bias from review volume disparities across brands.
  • Performed PCA (Principal Component Analysis) to identify complaint dimensions: PC1(Trouble/Irritation), PC2(Dryness), segmenting brands into High/Low-negativity clusters.
Data-Driven Brand Insight Uncovered critical misalignments between brand messaging and consumer reality. For example, Bioderma's 'ultra-moisturizing' claim contradicted the most frequent complaint: lack of hydration. Demonstrated how quantitative text analysis can validate or challenge brand positioning.
Sequential Elimination Framework Applied consumer psychology theory (Sequential Elimination) to marketing strategy. By mapping complaint priorities, proposed that brands should first eliminate negative triggers before emphasizing positive features, flipping the traditional feature-first approach.
